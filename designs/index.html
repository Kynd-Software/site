The ADHD experience

Productivity apps weren't built for how you think

Most apps assume you can just… start. They don't account for executive dysfunction, time blindness, or the overwhelm that comes with too many choices.

😶‍🌫️

Task paralysis

Staring at a 40-item to-do list, completely unable to pick where to start — even when the deadline is real.

⏳

Time blindness

Hours vanish. Every deadline feels like a surprise, even the ones you wrote down.

🌀

Shame spirals

Missing one day breaks the streak, and suddenly you've abandoned the whole system — again.

kynd was designed to change all of this.

What's inside

Everything your brain actually needs

No complexity, no overwhelm. kynd gives you the tools that genuinely work for ADHD minds — flexible, forgiving, and kind.

Focus Mode

One task. Full screen. Everything else gone. When the world is too loud, kynd creates a quiet corner for just the thing that matters right now.

Learn more

Energy-Aware Scheduling

Tell kynd how you're feeling. It matches tasks to your energy level — no more forcing deep work when you're running on empty.

Smart Task Capture

Voice, text, or photo — capture tasks however your brain is working in that moment. kynd organises them later.

Body Doubling

Work alongside others in live virtual sessions. The presence of another person — even in silence — can make starting feel possible.

Streak-Free Wins

kynd celebrates what you did do — not what you didn't. No punishment for off days. Every win counts, no matter how small.

Routine Builder

Gentle, flexible routines that adapt when life gets messy. Because it will. And that's okay.

Getting started

Three steps to a calmer day

Getting started with kynd takes minutes. You don't need a perfect system — you just need a beginning.

1

Tell kynd about you

Share how you work best, what your energy patterns look like, and what you're trying to get done. No lengthy questionnaire — just a friendly conversation.

2

kynd learns your patterns

Over time, kynd notices when you're at your best, what helps you focus, and where you get stuck — and adapts to you, not the other way round.

3

Celebrate every win

kynd gently guides you through your day and celebrates progress — big, small, and everything in between. No streaks to break. No shame.

Designed differently

Built with the ADHD brain in mind

kynd isn't a generic productivity app with an ADHD label stuck on. Every design decision — from the colour palette to the notifications — was made with neurodivergent users at the centre.

No shame, ever

kynd never uses language that makes you feel bad for being human. Missed a day? The app starts fresh with you, without comment.

Low-overwhelm design

Never more than three things asking for your attention at once. The interface is calm by design — generous white space, clear hierarchy, no decision fatigue.

Adapts to real life

Plans fall apart. That's fine. kynd recalibrates without drama — sliding tasks forward, adjusting priorities, keeping you moving.

Research-backed

kynd is built with guidance from ADHD specialists, occupational therapists, and — most importantly — people with ADHD who told us what actually helps.

Celebrate small wins

Getting dressed. Sending one email. Making the call. kynd recognises that for an ADHD brain, these are real achievements worth celebrating.

Gentle nudges, not alarms

Notifications that feel supportive rather than demanding. kynd checks in, it doesn't bark orders.
